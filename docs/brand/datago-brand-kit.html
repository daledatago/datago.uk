11 · USAGE & ALTERNATIVES

Rules & the three marks I considered

Do. Pair Datago's mark with the wordmark in primary uses. The mark alone is allowed at >64px (avatars, app icons).
Do. Keep the wordmark lowercase to match the family pattern (datago, bridgly, agents · pencils).
Do. Use cobalt at <10% of any surface. The mark's intersection square is the single largest brand-coloured shape that should appear in a layout.
Do. Use the D-tile for ≤32px contexts (favicon, OS tray). Keep the two-square mark for ≥40px (lockup, hero, OG).
Don't. Mix Datago's cobalt with Bridgly's green in the same lockup. Parent-product visual co-presence happens through layout, not through colour mixing.
Don't. Use the mark on photography without a 12% black scrim. The two-square geometry needs a clean base.
Don't. Use the strapline as a headline. "Governed organisational intelligence products" is the corporate descriptor — it sits below the mark on letterheads and OG, never replaces a section heading.
Don't. Make the Lab brand louder than Bridgly. The corporate path is Buyer → Datago → Bridgly; the lab is intentionally on the side.
