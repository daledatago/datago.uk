Thinking · hero graphic

Three concepts for the hero abstraction

The OG image you've described is "Abstract operating intelligence network." That's a strong frame — but it can be expressed three different ways depending on which dimension you want to lead with. The mockup above uses Concept A. Here's what each one says.

A · used in hero ✓

The Operating Mesh

Three horizontal layers — systems, intelligence, decisions — with nodes and connections. Brand-coloured middle layer is the operating-intelligence layer. Active brand-coloured paths show decisions flowing through governance.

Says: we're the layer between source systems and the decisions that get made. Best read for buyers who already get the architecture concept.
B · alternative

The Concentric Layer

Outer ring = source systems. Middle ring = intelligence. Inner core = governed decisions. Surrounding nodes connect inward. The operating layer literally sits at the centre.

Says: governance is the protected core. Most "trustworthy" feel. Best for governance-led buyer conversations.
C · alternative

The Sankey Flow

Three pillars side-by-side. Curved volume-weighted flows show how source signals become intelligence, then decisions. Most data-vis-literate visual.

Says: volume of flow matters, governance is throughput-aware. Best for ops-leaders / finance buyers who think in flows.

Recommendation. Start with A · Operating Mesh on the home hero. It's the most flexible for marketing copy (works with "we connect" / "we govern" / "we measure" framings). Keep B and C as concept-spread pages or section visuals later on.
