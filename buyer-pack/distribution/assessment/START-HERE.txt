Suggested three-minute review
1. Open Create questions and inspect the approved sources and assessment intent.
2. Prepare the three-question draft set and inspect it in the filtered question bank.
3. Open one record, compare the proposed answer with its source and checks, then record a review decision.
4. Use Question bank to search and filter the wider illustrative content set.
5. Open Reporting to inspect workflow, review-reason and item-evidence views.

The question and review record are illustrative. They are not approved CITB test content and require construction subject-matter and assessment-specialist review.
